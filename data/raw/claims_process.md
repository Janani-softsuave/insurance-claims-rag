# ABC Insurance — Claims Process Guide

---

## How to File a Claim

### Step 1 — Notify the Insurer Immediately
Report the incident to ABC Insurance within **24 hours** for theft claims and
within **7 days** for all other claims. You can notify via:
- **Toll-free helpline:** 1800-XXX-XXXX (24 × 7)
- **Mobile app:** "ABC Insurance" → Raise a Claim
- **Email:** claims@abcinsurance.in
- **Nearest branch office**

Failure to notify within the stipulated period may result in the claim being
**repudiated** unless a valid reason for delay is provided.

### Step 2 — File an FIR (where applicable)
An FIR (First Information Report) with the local police is **mandatory** for:
- Theft of vehicle or property
- Third-party bodily injury or death
- Riots, malicious damage
- Burglary

For accidents involving only own-damage with no third-party injury, an FIR is
**not mandatory** but is recommended.

### Step 3 — Document the Loss
Collect and preserve:
- Photographs of damaged vehicle/property from multiple angles
- Repair estimates from authorised service centres or contractors
- Medical bills and reports (for personal accident claims)
- Witness statements, if available

### Step 4 — Submit the Claim Form
Complete the **Claim Intimation Form** and submit within **15 days** of the incident
along with the following documents:

**For Motor Claims:**
- Duly signed claim form
- Copy of Registration Certificate (RC)
- Copy of valid Driving Licence
- Copy of insurance policy / certificate
- FIR copy (if applicable)
- Repair estimate from garage

**For Property Claims:**
- Duly signed claim form
- Copy of insurance policy
- Photographs of damage
- Repair estimate / contractor's quote
- FIR copy (if applicable)
- Ownership proof (sale deed / rent agreement)

### Step 5 — Surveyor Appointment
ABC Insurance will appoint a **licensed surveyor** within **48 hours** of receiving
the claim. The surveyor will:
- Inspect the damaged property or vehicle
- Assess the extent of loss
- Submit a survey report to the insurer within **30 days**

For claims below ₹50,000, a **spot survey** may be conducted via video call.

### Step 6 — Claim Settlement
- Once the survey report and all documents are received, ABC Insurance will settle
  the claim within **30 days**.
- Payment is made by **NEFT** directly to the insured's registered bank account
  or to the repairer / garage (cashless claims).
- For **cashless motor claims**, the vehicle must be taken to a **network garage**.
  A list of network garages is available on the website and mobile app.

## Cashless vs Reimbursement Claims

| Feature | Cashless | Reimbursement |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| Garage type | Network garage only | Any garage |
| Payment | Direct to garage | Insured pays first, then claims back |
| Paperwork | Minimal | Full documents required |
| Settlement time | Faster (24–72 hours) | Up to 30 days |

## Common Reasons for Claim Rejection

1. **Policy lapse** — premium not paid, grace period expired.
2. **Non-disclosure** — material facts concealed at policy inception.
3. **Excluded peril** — damage caused by an event not covered (e.g. mechanical breakdown).
4. **Driving under influence** — DUI at the time of the accident.
5. **Invalid driving licence** — expired, wrong class, or no licence.
6. **Fraudulent claim** — misrepresentation of facts or inflated bills.
7. **Delayed intimation** — not notifying within the required period without valid reason.

## Grievance Redressal

If dissatisfied with the claim settlement:
1. Contact the **Grievance Cell** at grievance@abcinsurance.in within **15 days**.
2. If unresolved in 15 days, escalate to the **Insurance Ombudsman** in your region.
3. IRDAI's integrated grievance portal: **igms.irda.gov.in**
