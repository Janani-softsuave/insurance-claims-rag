# ABC Insurance — Property Exclusions Schedule (Endorsement Version 2025-R1)

**Endorsement version:** 2025-R1
**Effective from:** 2025-01-01
**Effective to:** current
**Supersedes:** Endorsement Version 2023-R1 (effective 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31)
**Applies to:** Building Cover and Contents Cover (Section 3 of the Policy Overview)

> **Important — this version narrows flood cover relative to 2023-R1.** Flood and
> inundation damage is now excluded from standard Building/Contents Cover by
> default and requires the Flood Extension Rider (IAP-20). A loss dated before
> 2025-01-01 must be assessed against Endorsement Version 2023-R1, not this
> document, regardless of which version the policyholder's current renewal
> carries.

---

## Clause 9 — Water, Flood and Inundation (renumbered and rewritten from 2023-R1 Clause 7)

### Clause 9(a) — Exclusion: Flood and Inundation
Flood and inundation damage to the insured Building and Contents **is excluded**
from standard Building Cover and Contents Cover, whether the event is sudden,
accidental, or gradual. This reverses the default position under the
superseded 2023-R1 Clause 7(a).

### Clause 9(b) — Flood Extension Rider (IAP-20)
Flood and inundation cover is available only where the policy carries the
**IAP-20 Flood Extension Rider**, taken as a separate endorsement with its own
additional premium and its own sub-limit shown on the Declarations Page. IAP-20
does not reinstate cover for gradual seepage (see Clause 9(c)).

### Clause 9(c) — Exclusion: Gradual Seepage (unchanged from 2023-R1 Clause 7(c))
Damage arising from gradual seepage, rising damp, or slow water ingress over
more than 72 hours remains excluded under both this version and the superseded
2023-R1, with or without IAP-20.

## Clause 11 — Fire and Allied Perils (unchanged from base policy, reproduced for completeness)

Covered as per Section 3.1 of the Policy Overview: fire, lightning, explosion,
riot and strike, storm, earthquake, and impact damage.
