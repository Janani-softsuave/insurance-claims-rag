# ABC Insurance — Comprehensive Motor & Property Policy Overview

**Policy Series:** CMP-2024 · **Effective:** 1 January 2024 · **Issuer:** ABC Insurance Ltd

---

## Section 1 — Who Is Covered

The policy covers the **named insured** and any **additional insured** listed on the
Declarations Page. Family members residing in the same household are automatically
covered under personal-lines motor policies.

---

## Section 2 — Motor Insurance Coverage

### 2.1 Third-Party Liability
Covers legal liability to third parties for:
- Bodily injury or death arising from use of the insured vehicle.
- Property damage caused to a third party's vehicle or property.
- Legal defence costs up to the policy limit.

**Minimum cover:** ₹15 lakh per accident as required by the Motor Vehicles Act.

### 2.2 Own-Damage (Comprehensive)
Covers accidental damage, fire, theft, natural calamities (flood, earthquake,
cyclone), malicious damage, and riots. **Not covered:** normal wear and tear,
mechanical or electrical breakdown, consequential loss.

### 2.3 Personal Accident Cover
- Accidental death or permanent total disability of the owner-driver: **₹15 lakh**.
- Hospital cash benefit: **₹2,000 per day** up to 30 days.

---

## Section 3 — Property Insurance Coverage

### 3.1 Building Cover
Covers the structure against fire, lightning, explosion/implosion, aircraft damage,
riot and strike, storm, flood, inundation, earthquake, and impact damage.
**Basis of settlement:** Reinstatement value (new for old).

### 3.2 Contents Cover
Covers household contents including furniture, electronics, appliances, and
personal effects. **Basis of settlement:** Market value at time of loss.

### 3.3 Burglary & Theft
Covers loss of contents due to forcible and violent entry. Unforced entry is
**not covered**. Cash limit: ₹25,000 per incident.

---

## Section 4 — Policy Limits and Deductibles

| Coverage | Sum Insured | Compulsory Deductible |
|----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Third-Party Liability | Unlimited (BI) / ₹7.5 lakh (PD) | Nil |
| Own-Damage Motor | As declared | ₹1,000 (private car) |
| Building | As declared | 1% of claim min ₹10,000 |
| Contents | As declared | ₹2,500 |
| Burglary & Theft | As declared | ₹5,000 |

A **voluntary deductible** of ₹2,500, ₹5,000, or ₹10,000 may be chosen for a premium
discount of 10%, 15%, or 20% respectively.

---

## Section 5 — Policy Period & Renewal

- Motor policies: **1 year**, renewable annually.
- Property policies: **1 year**, renewable annually.
- Long-term motor (2 or 3 years) available for new vehicles at a 5% discount.
- Coverage lapses if the premium is not paid within the **15-day grace period**.
