# ABC Insurance — Endorsements & Policy Riders (Addendum Pack 2024)

Endorsements modify the standard policy wording. Each endorsement is identified
by a unique **IMT** (India Motor Tariff) or **IAP** (India Addendum Pack) number.

---

## Motor Endorsements

### IMT-28 — Nil Depreciation (Zero Dep) Cover
- **What it does:** Removes depreciation deduction on plastic, rubber, fibre,
  and metal parts during own-damage claims. You receive the **full repair cost**
  without depreciation cuts.
- **Eligibility:** Vehicles up to **5 years old**; maximum **2 claims** per policy year.
- **Additional premium:** Approximately 20–25% of own-damage premium.
- **Not included:** Engine damage due to water ingression, mechanical/electrical failure,
  tyres and tubes (unless the vehicle is also damaged in the same accident).

### IMT-29 — Engine & Gearbox Protection
- **What it does:** Covers damage to the engine and gearbox caused by water
  ingression (flooding), hydrostatic lock, or oil leakage due to an accident.
- **Eligibility:** Comprehensive policy holders only.
- **Note:** Applicable only when the vehicle is stationary or driven through flood water.
  Driving into a flooded area knowingly may lead to repudiation.

### IMT-34 — Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover
- **What it does:** In case of total loss or theft, the insurer pays the **original
  invoice value** of the vehicle (including registration fees and road tax),
  rather than the Insured Declared Value (IDV).
- **Eligibility:** Vehicles up to **3 years old**.
- **Benefit:** Bridges the gap between IDV and on-road price.

### IMT-40 — Roadside Assistance (RSA)
- **Services covered:** Towing (up to 50 km), flat tyre change, emergency fuel
  delivery, battery jump-start, key retrieval, and arrangement of alternative transport.
- **Limit:** 4 assistance events per policy year.
- **Exclusions:** Mechanical breakdown not resulting from an accident.

### IMT-47 — Consumables Cover
- **What it does:** Covers the cost of consumables — nuts, bolts, washers, engine
  oil, coolant, AC gas — that are normally excluded from a standard claim.
- **Note:** Must be availed at a network garage.

## Property Endorsements

### IAP-01 — Earthquake (Fire & Shock) Extension
- Extends the standard Fire policy to cover **direct loss or damage** caused by
  earthquake including fire, shock, and subsequent landslide.
- **Deductible:** 5% of claim amount, minimum ₹25,000.

### IAP-07 — Terrorism Cover
- Covers physical damage to the insured property arising from acts of **terrorism**
  as defined by the Terrorism Risk Insurance Pool (TRIP).
- **Exclusion:** War, civil war, nuclear/chemical/biological attacks.
- Underwritten by the **National Insurance Pool** at IRDAI-approved rates.

### IAP-12 — Rent for Alternative Accommodation
- If the insured building is rendered **uninhabitable** due to an insured peril,
  this endorsement pays actual rent for alternative accommodation up to:
  - **₹25,000 per month** for up to **6 months**, or until repairs are complete,
    whichever is earlier.

### IAP-15 — Landlord's Legal Liability
- Covers a landlord's legal liability to a **tenant** for bodily injury or property
  damage caused by a defect in the insured building.
- Limit: **₹10 lakh** per occurrence.

## How to Add an Endorsement

Endorsements can be added:
- **At inception:** When taking a new policy.
- **Mid-term:** Any time during the policy period; pro-rata premium applies.
- Contact your agent, visit a branch, or use the ABC Insurance app → "Modify Policy".

Endorsements take effect on the date of payment confirmation. Backdated endorsements
are **not permitted**.

---

## Key Exclusions Across All Endorsements

- Loss or damage whilst the vehicle is used outside the **geographical area** stated
  in the policy (India, unless extended).
- Consequential loss of any kind.
- Loss due to **depreciation** (unless IMT-28 is attached).
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Nuclear perils, war, and invasion.
