**Effective from:** 2023-01-01
**Effective to:** 2024-12-31 (superseded by Endorsement Version 2025-R1)
**Applies to:** Building Cover and Contents Cover (Section 3 of the Policy Overview)

## Clause 7 — Water, Flood and Inundation

### Clause 7(a) — Standard Cover
Flood and inundation damage to the insured Building and Contents **is covered**
under the standard Building Cover and Contents Cover described in Section 3,
without need for a separate endorsement, subject to Clauses 7(b) and 7(c) below.

### Clause 7(b) — Sudden and Accidental Events
Cover under Clause 7(a) applies where the flood or inundation results from a
**sudden and accidental** event — river overflow, cyclonic rainfall, storm
surge, or municipal drainage failure. The event need not be declared a natural
disaster for cover to apply.

### Clause 7(c) — Exclusion: Gradual Seepage
Damage arising from **gradual seepage, rising damp, or slow water ingress over
more than 72 hours** is excluded, since such loss is treated as a maintenance
matter rather than an insured peril. This is the only exclusion under this
Clause; a flood loss that is sudden and accidental is covered in full subject
to the standard Building/Contents deductible in Section 4 of the Policy
Overview.

## Clause 9 — Fire and Allied Perils (unchanged from base policy, reproduced for completeness)

Covered as per Section 3.1 of the Policy Overview: fire, lightning, explosion,
riot and strike, storm, earthquake, and impact damage.
