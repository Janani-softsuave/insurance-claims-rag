# ABC Insurance — Claims FAQ

## General Claims

**Q: How soon must I report a claim?**
A: For theft, report within 24 hours. For all other claims (accident, fire, flood,
burglary), report within 7 days of the incident. Delayed reporting may lead to
repudiation unless you provide a valid written reason for the delay.

**Q: Can I get a claim settled in cash?**
A: Yes, via the reimbursement route. You pay the repairer, then submit original
bills to ABC Insurance. Settlement is by NEFT to your registered bank account
within 30 days of document submission. Alternatively, use a network garage for a
cashless settlement where we pay the garage directly.

**Q: What is IDV and how does it affect my claim?**
A: IDV (Insured Declared Value) is the current market value of your vehicle and
represents the maximum amount payable in case of total loss or theft. IDV is
calculated as the ex-showroom price minus depreciation based on vehicle age:
- Up to 6 months: 5% depreciation
- 6 months to 1 year: 15%
- 1–2 years: 20%
- 2–3 years: 30%
- 3–4 years: 40%
- 4–5 years: 50%
Vehicles over 5 years: IDV is mutually agreed upon.

**Q: What is a total loss claim?**
A: A claim is declared a **total loss** when the cost of repair exceeds 75% of the
IDV. In this case, the insurer pays the IDV (or invoice value if RTI endorsement
IMT-34 is active) and takes possession of the salvage.

**Q: My claim was rejected. What can I do?**
A: 1) Request a written rejection letter with reasons. 2) Submit a representation
to the Grievance Cell at grievance@abcinsurance.in within 15 days. 3) If unresolved,
approach the Insurance Ombudsman in your city. 4) File a complaint on IRDAI's IGMS
portal (igms.irda.gov.in).

## Motor Claims

**Q: My car was damaged in a flood. Is it covered?**
A: Yes, if you hold a **Comprehensive (Own-Damage)** policy. Flood is a natural
calamity and is covered. Engine damage due to water ingression is covered only if
you have the **Engine & Gearbox Protection** endorsement (IMT-29). Without it,
engine hydrostatic lock is excluded.

**Q: Can I take my car to any garage?**
A: For **reimbursement** claims, yes — any registered garage. For **cashless**
settlement, the vehicle must go to an **ABC network garage**. A list is available
on the ABC Insurance app and website.

**Q: My windshield cracked. Do I need to pay a deductible?**
A: If you have **glass breakage** cover, the windshield replacement is settled
without the standard compulsory deductible of ₹1,000, provided no other damage
is claimed simultaneously.

**Q: I have Zero Dep cover (IMT-28). Will I get a full reimbursement?**
A: Yes, depreciation on most parts is waived. However, tyres, tubes, and batteries
carry a standard 50% depreciation regardless of IMT-28. Also, IMT-28 is capped at
2 claims per policy year.

**Q: My vehicle was stolen. What documents do I need?**
A: FIR from the police, Form 28/29/30 (NOC and transfer forms), original vehicle
keys, RC book, policy document, and a **Non-Traceable Certificate** from the police
after 90 days. The claim is settled only after the police close the trace.

## Property Claims

**Q: Is my home covered for earthquake damage?**
A: Standard fire policies **do not cover earthquake** by default. You need the
**Earthquake Extension** endorsement (IAP-01). If you have it, both the fire
following earthquake and the shock damage are covered.

**Q: My tenant's belongings were damaged in a fire. Am I liable?**
A: A standard building policy covers the structure only. Your tenant's contents
are the tenant's responsibility and should be covered under a separate contents
policy. However, if the fire originated from a defect in the building, the
**Landlord's Legal Liability** endorsement (IAP-15) would cover your liability.

**Q: My house is under renovation. Is it still covered?**
A: Minor renovation (painting, tiling) does not affect coverage. Major structural
work (demolishing walls, adding floors) must be **declared** to ABC Insurance.
Failure to declare may void the policy during the renovation period.

**Q: What is the depreciation applied to contents claims?**
A: Contents are settled on **market value** (not reinstatement value). Depreciation
depends on age and condition. Electronics older than 5 years typically attract
50% depreciation. Furniture and appliances are assessed by the appointed surveyor.

## Health & Personal Accident Claims

**Q: What is the deadline for submitting a health claim?**
A: For **planned hospitalisation**, notify ABC Insurance 48 hours before admission.
For **emergency hospitalisation**, notify within 24 hours of admission. Post-discharge
reimbursement claims must be submitted within **30 days** of discharge.

**Q: Is a pre-existing condition covered?**
A: Pre-existing conditions are covered after a **waiting period of 48 months**
(4 years) of continuous coverage with ABC Insurance. Specific diseases such as
cataracts and joint replacements have a **24-month waiting period**.

**Q: What is covered under the Personal Accident policy?**
A: The policy covers Accidental Death (100% sum insured), Permanent Total Disability
(100%), Permanent Partial Disability (as per schedule), and Temporary Total
Disability (1% of sum insured per week, up to 100 weeks). Medical expenses
following an accident are covered up to 40% of the sum insured.
